Yoru v3.17.4 — Activity Rank Ladder + Frakció Discord Role Quick Fix

ACTIVITY RANKS
- Replaced the old 7-step default Activity milestone ladder with a 15-step Hungarian social ladder:
  Lv5 Csöves
  Lv10 Pórnép
  Lv15 Közmunkás
  Lv20 Minimálbéres
  Lv30 Melós
  Lv40 Szakmunkás
  Lv50 Maszekos
  Lv60 Kft.-tulaj
  Lv75 Vállalkozó
  Lv90 Nagyvállalkozó
  Lv110 Újgazdag
  Lv130 Stróman
  Lv150 Oligarcha
  Lv175 Felső tízezer
  Lv200 NER-elit
- Milestone level/name layout is now persisted in the live guild DB/settings instead of being file-only.
- Existing manual Activity role mappings are migrated by exact role name where possible.
- Activity role sync can bind existing named roles and create missing configured milestone roles.
- 15 milestones remain under Discord's 25-option select limit.

FRAKCIÓ DISCORD ROLES
- Creating a Frakció now creates/adopts a Discord role named exactly after the Frakció.
- The role is hoisted so the Frakció appears as a separate group in the Discord member list.
- The founder receives the role immediately.
- Members receive the role when accepting an invite.
- Leaving/kicking removes the role; disbanding deletes it.
- Existing Frakciók are reconciled on startup: missing roles are created/adopted and current members are synced.
- Discord role IDs are persisted in the live DB, so full ZIP updates do not lose the mapping.

No economy balance changes.
